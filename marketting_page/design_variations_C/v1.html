Gearotons · M17 Series

All-in-one motion, catalogued.

Affordable and Simple All-in-One Motion Control. Motor, driver, controller, and encoder — one package, one RS-485 bus.

The M17 Series Servomotors are all-in-one motion control: motor, driver, motion controller, and encoder in a single compact package. RS-485 lets you daisy-chain any number of motors from one connection. Three models — M17-60, M17-48, M17-40 — share the same control characteristics at three torques.

01 — Capabilities

What is integrated.

Control from Mac, PC, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, or ESP32 with a low-cost RS-485 adapter. High-level commands, self-calibration, LED status, trapezoid profiles, closed-loop control, and comprehensive error handling. Standard NEMA 17 mounting, 12–24 V, a robust protocol. Education and industry: robotics, CNC, automated testing, scientific instruments.

03 — First move

One revolution, two seconds.

Python on any desktop. The same ideas exist in the Arduino library.

import servomotor
from servomotor import M3

servomotor.open_serial_port()
motor = M3("X", time_unit="seconds",
                position_unit="degrees")
motor.system_reset()
motor.enable_mosfets()
motor.trapezoid_move(360, 2)  # one revolution in 2 s

pip3 install servomotor · Arduino library: Library Manager → “Servomotor”

## Round 2 — company pastiches (v11–v40)

### v11 NVIDIA
Dark almost-black ground, NVIDIA-adjacent lime `#76B900` as the only accent (not Gearotons green). Full-bleed cinematic hero with a small uppercase kicker (“Physical AI · Motion”). Industry section tiles (Robotics / CNC / Science / Education) like NVIDIA’s industry carousels. News-card rhythm for features. All-caps 11–12px labels. Futura/Avenir/Helvetica Neue stack. Green pill CTAs on black. Footer is a dense link farm.

### v12 Apple
Stacked full-width product stories: white band, black band, light-gray band. Huge centered product shot, SF-like system stack (`-apple-system, "Helvetica Neue"`). Headline + one-line sub + pair of text links “Learn more / Shop”. Almost no card chrome, no shadows, no gradients. Specs as a quiet comparison strip. Code as a small footnote, not a terminal skin. Generous vertical padding (80–120px).

### v13 Alphabet (Google)
about.google energy: large white field, Product-Sans-like (`Arial, "Gill Sans", sans-serif`), Google-adjacent primary dots used as *tiny* decorative dots only (blue `#4285F4`, red `#EA4335`, yellow `#FBBC05`, green `#34A853`) — never as a logo. Soft 24–32px rounded image wells. 3-up story cards with big photos. Friendly headline (“Motion control, made simple.”). Pill buttons with light gray hover.

### v14 Microsoft
Fluent / microsoft.com: Segoe-like (`"Segoe UI", SegoeUI, Tahoma`). `#0078D4` primary. Deep mega-nav with many text links. Greeting-style hero (“Motion control for every bench”). Product *tiles* in a 2×2 then 4-up, each with photo + short title + “Shop now” link. Dense, commercial, slightly busy on purpose. Footer is a multi-column sitemap.

### v15 Amazon
Retail amazon.com more than aboutamazon: dark navy header `#131921`, search-bar *shape* used as a visual motif (not a working search), orange `#FF9900` CTA. Dense. Price-row *layout* used for spec rows (no prices). Breadcrumb. Yellow add-to-cart style Shop button. Feature bullets like a product detail page. Tabular “compare models” like a shopping comparison.

### v16 TSMC
Taiwanese semiconductor corporate: deep navy `#001F3F` / `#003366`, gold `#C5A46E` hairlines. Formal serif headlines (`Georgia, "Times New Roman"`). Process-node *rhythm* applied to motor models (three “nodes”). Full-width industrial photography. Reserved, bilingual-feeling section labels in small caps. Specs as a fab datasheet.

### v17 SpaceX
spacex.com: pure black, white Futura-like (`Futura, "Century Gothic", "Avenir Next", sans-serif`), full-viewport hero with transparent motor and huge wordmark-scale “M17”. Minimal top nav (white text, no fill). Secondary sections full-bleed photos with white type over a dark gradient. Almost no rounded corners. Shop is a thin white outline button that fills on hover.

### v18 Broadcom
Enterprise semiconductor: white, corporate blue `#0066A1`, lots of boxed product categories. Top utility bar. Hero is a short claim + “Explore products”. Feature blocks as icon-less category cards with right-arrows. Conservative radius (2px). Dense footer. Feels like a parts catalog for a purchasing manager.

### v19 Saudi Aramco
aramco.com: desert-to-teal corporate. Deep teal `#004C45`, sand `#E8D9C0`, white. Full-bleed photographic bands with large serif headlines. “At a glance” statistic row (only real facts: 32 kHz, 560 RPM, 12–24 V, 3 models). Magazine tiles (“Elements”-like). Calm, national-scale energy.

### v20 Tesla
tesla.com: extreme reduction. White, near-black type, very thin weights. Centered. Full-bleed product. Nav is small centered word + text links. CTA pair “Shop Now / Documentation” as small text or a single black pill. Specs as a vertical key-value list like a car spec strip. Almost no color. Letter-spacing on H1.

### v21 Meta
meta.com/about: oversized stacked statements that change the thought (“We’re building… / all-in-one motion.”). Soft blues and a horizon gradient (blue → violet, no Meta logo). Large photography, rounded-2xl. Human, connective. Shop as a black pill. Airy, 72–88px display type.

### v22 Samsung
samsung.com/us: dark cinematic product hero, “Learn more / Buy” pair (Buy = Shop Now). Full-width carousels of models as three product stages. Sharp sans. Black/graphite with a thin blue accent `#1428A0`. Feature sections titled like campaign lines. Large product-on-black cutouts.

### v23 Micron
micron.com: “invisible layer” B2B tech. Dark navy + electric cyan `#00A3E0`. Bento grid of product tiles (uneven 2+1). Datasheet / design-tools energy for the code + specs block. Industry strips (Robotics, CNC, Automation). Corporate but contemporary.

### v24 Berkshire Hathaway
berkshirehathaway.com: deliberately 1996. Times New Roman on `#FFFFFF`, default blue links `#0000EE`, visited `#551A8B`. Centered title, HR rules, unordered list of section jumps. No CSS layout beyond `max-width: 720px; margin: 0 auto; font-family: Times, serif`. Specs as a classic HTML table with border=1. This is the point.

### v25 Eli Lilly

### v26 SK hynix

### v27 JPMorgan Chase

cisco.com: Cisco blue `#049FD9` / `#0D274D`. Enterprise networking. “Bridge” metaphor for RS-485. Product family table. Partner/platform language (Arduino, Pi, ESP32, PC). Professional, slightly dense, solution-brief. Footer with legal-looking small type (no fake legal claims).

### v40 Caterpillar
caterpillar.com: CAT yellow `#FFCD11`, black, industrial machinery. Huge yellow rules. Bold condensed sans (`Impact, "Arial Narrow", sans-serif` used carefully). Construction-site photography energy on application photos. Models as three machines in a lineup. No-nonsense Shop button (black on yellow). Heavy, durable, outdoor.

## Quality bar (why C should beat B)

- Unique **page architecture** per variant, not a theme on one skeleton.
- Real type scale, spacing scale, hover states, and image treatment.
- System fonts only. No CDNs.
- Honest copy only. No prices, awards, customer counts, testimonials.
- Dark pages use transparent cutouts; light pages use studio JPEGs.

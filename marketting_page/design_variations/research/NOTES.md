# Design study — Top 30 companies by market cap (July 2026)

Screenshots in `research/shots/` captured 2026-07-07 with headless Chrome at 1440×2400.
Each entry ends with "→ For Gearotons:" — the concrete design translation for the M17 variant.

Variant numbering: v11 = rank 1 (NVIDIA) … v40 = rank 30 (Caterpillar).

---

## 1. NVIDIA (nvidia.com) → v11
- Palette: white page, black text, signature green #76B900 (astonishingly close to Gearotons #7AB648!). Dark charcoal footer/sections.
- Type: proprietary geometric sans (NVIDIA Sans), bold weights, sentence case. Big left-aligned section heads ("Artificial Intelligence", "Design and Simulation").
- Layout: dense but ordered. Top news carousel with tab-strip underneath (small category label + 2-line headline, active tab gets green underline bar). Full-width promo band with faint triangular/polygonal line pattern in background (their triangle motif) + green logo lockup.
- Cards: square-ish image cards in 2-3 col grids; image top, category eyebrow "Artificial Intelligence | Blog", bold headline, small body. Sharp corners (0-2px radius). Green square arrow buttons for carousel prev/next.
- CTAs: solid green rectangular buttons with black/white text ("Learn More" + external-link icon), green underlined text links "Get Your Pass Now >".
- Feel: engineering powerhouse; green-on-dark accents; angular geometry; category-driven content grid.
→ For Gearotons: white + black + #76B900-style green; left-aligned bold section heads; tab-strip carousel of "news" (features); triangular line-pattern promo band; sharp-cornered cards with eyebrow labels; solid green rect CTAs. Perfect brand-color match — lean in hard.

## 2. Apple (apple.com) → v12
- Palette: white/very-light gradients (ice blue), black text, blue links/CTAs (#0071e3); one black tile section for contrast (MacBook Pro).
- Type: SF Pro; huge medium-weight display headlines, centered; short subline; two CTAs side by side: solid blue pill "Learn more" + outline blue pill "Buy".
- Layout: vertical stack of full-bleed product "tiles", each 100vh-ish: headline top-center, product photo enormous below; then a 2-up grid of half-width tiles (black tile + white tile).
- No visible borders, no cards with strokes — tiles are separated by background color changes only. Nav is thin translucent bar with tiny text links.
- Feel: product photography is the entire design; extreme restraint; generous negative space.
→ For Gearotons: stacked full-bleed tiles, one per idea (hero motor / daisy-chain / code / kit), centered display type, blue→green pill CTA pairs ("Learn more" solid green, "Buy" outline), 2-up half tiles (one black one white) for M17-60/M17-40, thin translucent nav. (Note: close to v10 but tile-stack + 2-up grid + CTA-pair pattern makes it distinctly Apple-homepage.)

## 3. Alphabet / Google (about.google) → v13
- Palette: white nav, full-BLACK hero band, then white; Google blue #1a73e8 pill CTAs; 4-color logo is the only multicolor element; soft pastel blue blob shapes lower down.
- Type: Product Sans/Google Sans — light/regular weight, very large, sentence case. Huge centered light headline for section intros ("Gemini Spark updates").

→ For Gearotons: black hero band w/ floating rounded photo collage (motor, kit, adapter tiles at offsets), light-weight giant headlines, green pill CTAs, bordered rounded cards with corner arrow icons, pastel-green blob section. Use green instead of Google blue.

The M17 Series Servomotors: M17-60, M17-48, and M17-40

The M17 Series Servomotors are all-in-one motion control solutions that integrate a motor, motor driver, motion controller, and encoder in a single compact package. The RS-485 interface permits any number of motors to be daisy-chained and controlled from a single connection point. The motors may be controlled from a Mac, PC, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, or ESP32, requiring only a low-cost RS485 adapter. Standard NEMA 17 mounting dimensions apply. Operating voltage is 12 to 24 volts.

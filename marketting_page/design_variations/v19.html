Integration

Any number of motors.
One RS-485 connection.

Daisy-chain the whole machine and command it from a Mac, PC, Raspberry Pi, Arduino or ESP32. High-level instructions such as “Enable MOSFETs” and “Trapezoid move” replace timing-critical STEP/DIR signals — with standard NEMA 17 mounting and a wide 12–24 V range.

Precision

Closed-loop control,
32,000 times a second.

A built-in encoder and a PID loop running at 32 kHz hold position with quiet confidence — far more power-efficient than a conventional stepper, protected by integrated over-current, over-voltage and over-temperature safeguards, up to 560 RPM.

Documentation
quickstart.pyPython
import servomotor
from servomotor import M3

servomotor.open_serial_port()
motor = M3("X", time_unit="seconds",
                position_unit="degrees")
motor.system_reset()
motor.enable_mosfets()
motor.trapezoid_move(360, 2)  # one revolution in 2 s
